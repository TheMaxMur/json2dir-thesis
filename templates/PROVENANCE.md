# Template provenance

`thesis.typ` is a local adaptation of **benplate 0.1.0**, the thesis template
by Ben Dierks, published on 24 March 2026 under the MIT license.

- Catalog: https://typst.app/universe/package/benplate
- Repository: https://github.com/Nasenbaer39/benplate
- Pinned distribution: https://packages.typst.org/preview/benplate-0.1.0.tar.gz
- License: `benplate-LICENSE`

The adaptation retains the thesis/frontmatter/body/appendix/backmatter structure,
the burgundy accent color, chapter-opening rule, serif chapter headings, and
numbered academic apparatus. It replaces IBM Plex with fonts embedded in the
Typst CLI, removes the hydra, outrageous, and drafting dependencies, replaces
institutional frontmatter with explicitly fictional English text, and uses
single-sided chapter starts. The resulting template builds without downloads.

No real university, degree, examiner, or institutional approval is claimed.
